# **Section Two: Change Project Scope Definition** 

| Problem Analysis   *(Please share background into the problem your project is trying to solve and the reason behind your choice)*  |  1 in 10 people in Ebonyi state live with some form of physical, sensory and intellectual disability. For women and youth, this reality creates a severe trap of financial dependence, social shame, and exclusion.   In Nigeria, living with a disability is often treated as a limitation rather than a natural difference. In the farming and trading communities of Ikwo LGA, Ebonyi state these barriers are concrete. Most of the existing training centres do not accommodate adaptive needs, forcing families to carry disabled relatives as lifelong dependents. Social isolation happens quietly through omission, and despite widespread smartphone ownership, this population has been entirely left out of the digital commerce wave. To solve this, building a new, standalone disability centre is unsustainable; it costs too much to run and reinforces segregation. Creating a custom mobile app is equally unrealistic due to limited technical skills and poor internet infrastructure. Instead, Team Ability Beyond Disability layers practical solutions onto what already works. We are partnering with existing local centres to retrofit them for access rather than building from scratch. We teach digital entrepreneurship using simple, everyday tools like WhatsApp Business on smartphones people already own. Finally, we host public community exhibitions that dismantle stigma through visible proof of talent. This approach is low-cost, fast, and built to outlast the grant cycle.  |
