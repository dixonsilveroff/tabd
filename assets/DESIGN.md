Brutalist Design Theme

#0A0A0A (near-black) as primary text and background fill, 
#FFFFFF (white) as background and inverse text, and 
#0047FF (electric blue) and #FFA300 (Japanese Yellow) as the two accent colors, used sparingly — maximum one accent application each per section.

No gradients. Soft drop shadows and subtle, smooth animations and transitions. No rounded corners anywhere, sharp corners. No decorative illustrations or icons. No stock photography. No soft tones, pastels, or any color outside the three listed above.

Typography: Headlines use Barlow Condensed Black at 64–80pt, all caps, tight line height at 0.9–0.95. Subheadings use Barlow Condensed Bold at 36–44pt. Body text uses Inter Regular at 18–22pt. Labels and captions use Inter Medium at 12–14pt, uppercase, letter-spacing 3–4px. All text is left-aligned by default unless a centering instruction is explicitly stated per slide.
